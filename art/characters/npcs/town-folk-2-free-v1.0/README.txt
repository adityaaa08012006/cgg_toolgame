TOWN FOLK 2 FREE v1.0
3 pixel art villager NPCs from Town Folk 2, animated in four directions:
Potter, Errand Boy and Night Watchman. 6 sprite sheets.

Per character, in characters/<name>/:
    walk.png     the walk cycle
    attack.png   the WORK loop (named attack.png so it fits the same loader)

Every sheet: four rows (down, left, right, up), columns are the frames of
the loop. MANIFEST.csv lists the exact grid of every sheet.
