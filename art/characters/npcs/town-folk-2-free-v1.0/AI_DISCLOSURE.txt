AI DISCLOSURE
Town Folk 2 Free v1.0

HOW THESE ASSETS WERE MADE

  AI-generated, then curated, corrected, timed and sheeted by hand. Every
  figure was chosen from candidates and the rejects thrown away; every
  direction of every animation was watched one by one and either accepted or
  ordered again; frame timing was evened out, and the sheets were assembled
  to a single documented grid.

WHY WE SAY SO

  Some buyers do not want AI-generated art, and some storefronts require the
  disclosure. Both are legitimate. You should be able to decide before you
  spend money, not after.

  This pack is labelled as AI-generated on every store we sell it on.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOUR PROJECT

  The usage rights in LICENSE.txt apply regardless. Note that some game jams,
  storefronts and communities have their own rules about AI-generated content -
  check the rules of any place you intend to publish before you build on these
  assets.
